# 13. Worked operating-point example

This chapter demonstrates units and equations for the selected nominal
configuration. It is an analytical reference example, not a CFD prediction or
an experimental record.

## 13.1 Inputs

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---:|
| Diameter $D$ | 0.240 m |
| Blade count $Z$ | 5 |
| Pitch ratio $P/D$ | 1.20 |
| Expanded-area ratio $A_E/A_0$ | 0.75 |
| Rotational rate $n$ | 10 rev/s = 600 rpm |
| Advance coefficient $J$ | 0.80 |
| Density $\rho$ | 1000 kg/m$^3$ |
| Kinematic viscosity $\nu$ | $1.0\times10^{-6}$ m$^2$/s |

## 13.2 Advance speed and angular velocity

From $J=V_A/(nD)$:

$$
V_A=JnD=(0.80)(10)(0.240)=1.92\ \mathrm{m/s}.
$$

Angular speed:

$$
\Omega=2\pi n=62.8319\ \mathrm{rad/s}.
$$

Tip circumferential speed:

$$
U_{\mathrm{tip}}=\Omega R=\pi nD=7.54\ \mathrm{m/s}.
$$

These values match the scale and inlet velocity encoded in the baseline case.

## 13.3 B-series polynomial reference

The CSV generated by
[`generate_reference_curve.py`](../02_Geometry/generate_reference_curve.py)
gives at $J=0.8$:

$$
K_T=0.246536,\qquad
10K_Q=0.485673,\qquad
K_Q=0.0485673,\qquad
\eta_0=0.646318.
$$

Use the stored `10KQ` column carefully: divide by ten before inserting it as
$K_Q$ into the efficiency equation.

## 13.4 Dimensional loads

$$
T=K_T\rho n^2D^4
=0.246536(1000)(10^2)(0.240^4)
\approx81.79\ \mathrm N,
$$

$$
Q=K_Q\rho n^2D^5
=0.0485673(1000)(10^2)(0.240^5)
\approx3.867\ \mathrm{N\,m}.
$$

Then

$$
P_D=2\pi nQ\approx242.99\ \mathrm W,
\qquad
P_T=TV_A\approx157.05\ \mathrm W,
$$

$$
\eta_0=P_T/P_D\approx0.6463.
$$

The dimensional load scales as $\rho n^2D^4$ for thrust and
$\rho n^2D^5$ for torque at fixed coefficients. Doubling diameter at fixed
$n$, $J$, and geometry scales thrust by $2^4=16$ and torque by $2^5=32$ in
ideal nondimensional similarity. In a real model, Reynolds and cavitation
similarity can break that simple scaling.

## 13.5 Reynolds mismatch

Near $0.75R$, a rough chord estimate from the B-series radial planform law is
$c\approx0.0741$ m. Ignoring induced velocity:

$$
W_{0.75R}\approx
\sqrt{V_A^2+(\Omega\,0.75R)^2}
\approx5.97\ \mathrm{m/s},
$$

$$
Re_{0.75R}\approx\frac{cW}{\nu}
\approx4.42\times10^5.
$$

This is not the equivalent $Re=2\times10^6$ condition associated with the
polynomial reference. The estimate also uses approximate chord and ignores
induced flow. This mismatch is one reason the example is an arithmetic
demonstration, not a direct validation point.

## 13.6 Reproducibility check

Recompute $\eta_0$ two ways:

$$
\eta_0=\frac{J}{2\pi}\frac{K_T}{K_Q}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\eta_0=\frac{TV_A}{2\pi nQ}.
$$

They should agree to rounding if the definitions and units are consistent.
